Laser Composite Projection System

Projector Stand System & Nesting Optimization

Project Objective

This project focused on bridging the gap between digital composite design and physical manufacturing. After generating manufacturable flat ply geometries through the composites design workflow, the next challenge was accurately transferring those shapes onto composite fabric for cutting and layup.

Traditional methods relied on printed templates, manual measurements, or rough estimates, which introduced alignment errors, additional material usage, poor cosmetic surface, and excessive material waste.

To solve this, I designed and built a custom system capable of projecting full-scale flattened ply geometries directly onto composite fabric. This allowed plies to be cut precisely while maintaining proper fiber orientation and reducing manufacturing inconsistencies.

In addition, a custom nesting software workflow was developed to optimize material utilization by arranging ply geometries efficiently while respecting fiber rosette constraints and manufacturing settings.

📽️ Projector Stand System

Precision alignment · storable design · full-scale projection

The projector stand was designed specifically for precision layup methods, eliminating the need for printed paper templates and dramatically reducing material waste. Built with workshop constraints in mind, the stand delivers stable projection alignment across large composite molds while remaining easily storable or disassemblable into a 2D plane.

Key design considerations included:

  • Stable projection alignment
  • Must be easily storable, or dissassembleable into a 2D plane
  • Calibration accuracy for tilt and pan
  • Large area coverage (at least bigger than width of roll)
  • Manufacturability within workshop constraints

The system enables flattened ply geometries to be projected directly from CAD generated outputs onto fiberglass and carbon fiber fabrics at full scale. This significantly improves cutting precision, repeatability between parts, manufacturing speed, material utilization, and consistency between CAD design and final manufactured components.

Nesting Optimization

Python-based automated ply arrangement · fiber‑aware rotation

To further improve manufacturing efficiency, a custom nesting workflow was developed to automatically arrange flattened ply geometries. The nesting software is developed in Python, using heuristic packing algorithms while considering:

  • Fiber rosette direction (rotate only 0° or 180° to respect strength requirements)
  • Material boundaries – staying within roll width (914.4 mm)
  • Ply spacing requirements – adjustable separation for cutting tolerance
  • Manufacturability constraints – avoiding crowding and maintaining handleable sections

The algorithm reads DXF files exported from CATIA Composites Design, then iteratively arranges plies to maximize material utilization. The workflow includes geometry parsing, rotation and collision analysis, configurable nesting parameters, and automated export of projection-ready DXF and PDF cutting layouts for composite fabrication.
